Privacy Policy — YouTube Scroll Volume

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Overview

YouTube Scroll Volume is a browser extension that lets users adjust the volume of YouTube videos by scrolling their mouse wheel over the video player.

The extension is designed to collect as little information as possible. It does not operate an advertising, analytics, or user-tracking service.

Information the Extension Stores

YouTube Scroll Volume stores the following preferences:

Volume increment: The percentage by which video volume changes when scrolling.

Indicator duration: How long the on-screen volume indicator remains visible.

Indicator visibility: Whether the on-screen volume indicator is enabled.

These preferences are stored using the browser's extension storage functionality (chrome.storage.sync) so that the extension can remember the user's choices.

Depending on the browser and its sync settings, these preferences may be synchronized through the browser provider's sync service.

How Information Is Used

The stored preferences are used only to provide the extension's features and remember the user's configuration.

The extension does not collect or store:

YouTube watch history

Video titles or video content

General browsing history

Personal identification information

YouTube account credentials

Personal communications

The extension interacts with the YouTube video player to adjust its volume when the user scrolls over the player.

Information Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or share stored preferences with advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

The extension does not operate its own external analytics or tracking service. Browser-provided synchronization, when enabled and supported, is handled by the browser provider and is subject to that provider's privacy practices.

Data Security and Retention

Preferences remain in the browser's extension storage until changed or removed by the user, or until the extension's data is cleared or the extension is uninstalled.

Users can change their preferences through the extension popup. Browser sync behavior is controlled by the browser and the user's sync settings.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. Any updated version will be published at the same privacy policy address.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the support or contact information provided on the YouTube Scroll Volume Chrome Web Store listing.
